Third-party tools bundled with Orbix

- The Windows build workflow downloads the current x64 releases of FFmpeg Essentials, 7-Zip Extra, Pandoc, Deno, and yt-dlp from their upstream project release sites. Local build output is generated by `scripts/prepare-windows-tools.ps1`.
- FFmpeg is distributed under the license of the selected upstream build; the checked-in FFmpeg license is in tools/ffmpeg/LICENSE.
- 7-Zip is distributed under the GNU LGPL with unRAR restrictions and other notices; see tools/7zip/LICENSE.txt.
- Pandoc's license and copyright notices are in tools/pandoc/COPYING.txt and tools/pandoc/COPYRIGHT.txt.
- Deno's MIT license is in tools/deno/LICENSE.md. yt-dlp and its bundled dependencies' notices are in tools/yt-dlp/LICENSE.

Converter operations are performed locally. The Pandoc MSI is expanded into the current user's Orbix app data folder on first use; it is not installed system-wide.
